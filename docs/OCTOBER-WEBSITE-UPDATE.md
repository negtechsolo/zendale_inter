# October website update

## Existing approved wording

The approved business descriptions, service lists, mission, vision, values and existing page narratives remain unchanged. Existing homepage sections are retained; the six capability pillars move directly after Impact in Action and the partnership gateway moves below the new leadership/news previews.

Necessary changes to existing text:

- The network total changes automatically from 8 to 10 after adding Zendale Consulting and Zendale Central Store.
- Photograph alt text changes to accurately describe the supplied real images on the homepage, About, Corporate Healthcare, facility entries and Ikosi Ketu project.
- The old standalone homepage Ketu feature becomes one of four Impact slides. Its existing title and summary are preserved; the slider uses the new “View Project” action.

New text is confined to new network entries, leadership profiles based on supplied details, Impact and News pages, their navigation/actions, gallery captions and the Central Store quote journey.

## Assets

All new raster assets are WebP under `public/images/real/`; new URLs avoid the existing immutable image cache. Source photographs are at most 1600 pixels on the longest side; portraits and logos are at most 1000 pixels. Responsive variants and intrinsic dimensions are generated by `npm run images`. All 10 supplied logos retain transparent backgrounds and have empty outer padding cropped. No supplied faces or premises were regenerated.

Five original facility photo sets, Finnih exterior/team, four leadership portraits, Ikosi Ketu exterior/laboratory imagery and selected event/outreach photos are integrated. LifeCentre Med Support, Vhelar and Zendale Consulting use suitable existing representative service photographs. Central Store uses a generated pharmaceutical-supply illustration; all four retain official logos in the photo corner. Existing unrelated imagery elsewhere on the website remains outside this update.

## Supply quotes

- Page: `/supply-quote`, linked from Central Store’s homepage/network entry and profile.
- API: `POST /api/supply-quote`.
- Recipient is fixed server-side: `everester.oguh@zendalelimited.com`. Client requests cannot change it.
- Uses existing `RESEND_API_KEY` and `CONTACT_FROM` Vercel settings. `CONTACT_FROM` must be a verified sender in the connected Resend account. No new recipient environment variable is required; existing `/api/contact` behavior is unchanged.
- Supports up to 30 medicine rows and an optional PDF, CSV or TXT purchasing list up to 2 MB.
- Validates contact details, consent, rows and attachment constraints on the server. Includes a honeypot field.
- Confirmation appears only after Resend accepts the notification. Provider rejection or missing configuration returns an actionable error and retains the form.
- Automated tests mock the provider. No test email was sent to company staff.
